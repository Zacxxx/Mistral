# BuildShield AI
### AI Copilot for Independent Construction Professionals

---

## 1. One-Liner

An AI copilot for construction tradespeople that secures their quotes, protects their contracts, and stabilizes their cash flow in real time.

---

## 2. Problem

Independent contractors and small construction businesses fail not because of lack of skill, but because of:

- Underpriced quotes
- Missed cost items
- Contractual risk exposure
- Cash flow instability
- Poor documentation in disputes

They operate with:
- WhatsApp
- Excel
- Paper notes
- Fragmented tools

The result:
- Margin erosion
- Legal disputes
- Chronic financial stress
- Business failure within 3 to 5 years

---

## 3. Solution

BuildShield AI is a mobile-first AI operating layer for small construction businesses.

It focuses on three survival pillars:

1. Quote Intelligence
2. Contract Protection
3. Cash Flow Radar

Not an ERP.
Not a dashboard.
A survival engine.

---

## 4. Core Features

### 4.1 Intelligent Quote Engine

- Voice-to-quote generation
- Automatic detection of missing cost items
- Regional material price integration
- Margin simulation
- Risk scenario modeling

Output example:

> Estimated gross margin: 12%
> Risk exposure increases sharply if duration exceeds 18 days.

Goal: Prevent silent margin collapse.

---

### 4.2 Contract Risk Scanner

- Upload contract or client terms
- AI risk scoring
- Clause explanation in plain language
- Suggested protective amendments
- Penalty clause detection

Example output:

> Late penalty clause disproportionate to project value.
> Recommendation: add weather and supplier delay condition.

Goal: Reduce legal asymmetry.

---

### 4.3 Photo-Based Site Proof

- Daily photo upload
- Automatic categorization
- Timestamped documentation
- Progress classification
- Dispute-ready PDF generation

Goal: Build legal defense automatically.

---

### 4.4 Cash Flow Radar

- Incoming and outgoing projection
- Invoice aging detection
- Late payment alerts
- Burn rate estimation
- Liquidity survival countdown

Output example:

> Liquidity runway: 46 days if two invoices remain unpaid.

Goal: Prevent liquidity shock.

---

## 5. Target Users

Primary:
- Independent contractors

- Renovation specialists
- Subcontractors

Secondary:
- Trade associations
- Insurance providers
- Micro-finance institutions

---

## 6. Why This Works

Large firms have:
- ERP systems
- Legal departments
- Financial controllers

Small builders have:
- Operational skill
- Administrative vulnerability

This product addresses asymmetry, not productivity.

---

## 7. Market Thesis

Construction is one of the least digitized industries globally.
SMBs represent the majority of the sector by volume.

Even a 2 to 5% improvement in margin protection has massive financial impact.

Recurring subscription model:
29 to 59 USD per month

High retention due to:
- Embedded financial visibility
- Legal documentation archive
- Historical project data accumulation
